I've been learning Japanese for about a year and a half, originally just to read a novel I loved in its original language. Anki carried me through roughly 4,000 vocabulary words and I hit N2 reading without too much trouble — but my speaking and writing stayed stuck. That's the gap this project is about: output practice with the kind of personalized AI feedback that no static flashcard app can give.

The focus areas:
- **Personalized learning paths** — adapting to each learner's level and weak points
- **Rich AI feedback** — detailed explanations of errors, not just right/wrong
- **System design** — clean architecture that can grow with the feature set
