13 AI projects built during *Generative AI: Text and Image Synthesis Principles and Practice*, a course hosted by NCCU through the Taiwan AI College Alliance (TAICA). While smaller in scope than my main projects, each explores a different practical application — chatbots, RAG pipelines, multi-agent systems, and more.

Two standouts: a **prescription analysis system** where I scraped a traditional Chinese medicine knowledge base to build a multi-source RAG pipeline (rather than the manually-curated single-file approach the course suggested), and an **LLM Akinator benchmark** — using the 20-questions game format to evaluate multi-hop reasoning in large language models, an idea I came up with independently.

These projects informed the architecture decisions behind my larger work, particularly around RAG design and agentic workflows.
